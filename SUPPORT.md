# Getting help with WPMaven

- **The plugin:** please post in the [support forum on WordPress.org](https://wordpress.org/support/plugin/wpmaven/). Include your WordPress, WooCommerce and PHP versions, and the steps that show the problem.
- **How to do something:** the guides at [wpmaven.ai/docs](https://wpmaven.ai/docs).
- **Your account, billing, or a site hosted with WPMaven:** email support@wpmaven.ai.
- **A security issue:** please don't post it in public. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
