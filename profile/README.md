**WPMaven brings an AI team into WordPress.** It works on the site you already have, with your store, content, search visibility and messages in one place, and it can build a new site for your business.

### The plugin

- **Works right away, without an account:** an `llms.txt` file, structured data for your business and products, a count of the AI assistants reading your site, and an inbox for your contact forms.
- **With a free WPMaven account:** a chat with your AI team about your orders, customers, pages and posts. Every change is shown before it is applied, and you can undo it.
- **A new site:** describe your business, and WPMaven designs a site for you to host with WPMaven or publish on your own WordPress.

Install it from your WordPress admin (**Plugins > Add New Plugin**, then search for WPMaven) or from [WordPress.org](https://wordpress.org/plugins/wpmaven/).
